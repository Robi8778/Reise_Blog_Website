FAQ

What´s the story behind my travel blog?

Before the word "blog" or "weblog" was widely used, early digital travel writing functioned purely as online personal diaries. Travelers shared chronological stories, personal feelings, and raw notes with family and friends back home.

How do I plan, document, and share my travels?

Planning, documenting, and sharing a trip involves a simple, step-by-step process that keeps you organized and lets you bring others along on your journey!

How can brands, companies, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?
